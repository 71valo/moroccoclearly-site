For a slower trip

Choose fewer bases and protect arrival and departure time. Put one main activity into a day before adding optional stops. Check actual transport schedules before treating a route as feasible.

For food and culture

Choose the experiences you care about, then check opening times, booking needs and any dietary requirements directly. Leave unplanned time between fixed bookings.

When traveling with children

Build the plan around your group’s actual needs: sleeping arrangements, breaks, food, luggage and transfer requirements. Ask operators to confirm any essential equipment in writing.

For coast and outdoor time

Check conditions for the exact destination and activity near your travel date. Ask about equipment, supervision, cancellations and experience requirements. Do not assume a general seasonal guide is a forecast.

Choosing your travel dates

List your preferred activities and tolerance for heat, crowds and changes of plan. Compare those needs with destination-specific climate information and current calendars. Leave a weather alternative for outdoor bookings.

A simple itinerary check

  1. List each overnight base and fixed booking.
  2. Add the complete door-to-door journey, not just the main train or flight.
  3. Leave space for meals, check-in, delays and rest.
  4. Remove the least important stop if the day only works under perfect conditions.

Check train journeys with ONCF. Confirm other journeys with the relevant operator.
