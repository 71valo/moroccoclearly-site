Know what
you are reading.

Morocco, Clearly is an early travel-information project, starting with practical preparation for a first Marrakech visit.

Who is responsible?

Morocco, Clearly is written and edited by Omar Oujoud in Casablanca. A monitored contact address for corrections has not yet been published. We recognize that this limits accountability, and setting one up is a priority before a wider launch.

How this edition was prepared

The pages were prepared with AI assistance and online research. Relevant primary sources are linked in the guides. No local field verification, firsthand provider inspection or independent traveler testing is claimed.

The source-review date refers to reviewing online material. It is not a guarantee that a fare, rule or local condition remains unchanged. Suggested itineraries and preparation questions are editorial advice.

Corrections

There is no active public feedback or corrections form yet. We should publish a monitored contact and a correction log before asking visitors to submit reports. Until then, confirm important details directly with the named authority or provider.

What the current tools do with your inputs

The quote calculator performs its calculations in your browser. The site’s own code does not send those numbers to us. Checklist ticks are not intentionally saved between visits; use print or save as PDF if you want a copy. The copy button writes the displayed quote-request template to your clipboard when you press it.

There are no newsletter, account-registration, payment or review-submission forms in this edition. We have not added advertising trackers or analytics to the site’s own code. Fonts are served from this site itself, so there is no request to an outside font service.

Hosting and external links

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and is open to everyone without a sign-in. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have not completed our own review of GitHub’s logging, retention or processing locations. This page is a description of the current implementation, not a claim of completed privacy compliance.

When you follow a link to an external website, that site’s own privacy practices apply. Before adding subscriptions, feedback or other data collection, the project needs to identify the responsible operator, map the data flows and complete the applicable privacy requirements.

Commercial relationships

This edition has no paid listings, sponsored rankings, booking commissions or checkout. We do not make reservations or verify providers. If that changes, commercial relationships should be disclosed alongside the relevant recommendation and the legal requirements reviewed before the service starts.

Using the guides

Use them as preparation, then confirm time-sensitive details before acting. The calculator compares totals, not provider quality or fair market value. We do not assign safety ratings. For important travel decisions, consult current government advice and the provider’s written terms.
