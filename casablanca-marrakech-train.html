Casablanca to Marrakech by train

Direct trains run from Casa Voyageurs to Marrakech. What we have checked, and what to confirm for your own date.

The facts on this page were reviewed against their sources on 28 September 2026. None has been checked in person yet. Each fact shows its own status and date.

A dated example

ONCF’s timetable search for 28 September 2026 showed a direct train from Casa Voyageurs at 14:43, arriving 17:37 (2 h 54 min) Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources. This is one example, not a timetable for your date.

Fares

Search your date on ONCF’s booking site, oncf-voyages.ma, for the current fare.

Choosing a ticket type

ONCF sells non-flex, semi-flex and flex fares; non-flex cannot be exchanged or refunded Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources. Even with a flexible fare, an exchange depends on seats being available on the new train, and you pay any fare difference Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources.

Online e-tickets can be cancelled up to 30 minutes before departure Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources, under your fare’s conditions.

Our advice: if your flight could be late, or you have a fixed booking in Marrakech, a flexible fare may be worth the difference.

On the day

Your e-ticket can be shown on your phone or printed Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources. Luggage should fit within 1.2 m × 0.6 m × 0.4 m Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources.

Coming straight from the airport

Our advice: take the airport train to Casa Voyageurs and change there, and leave a generous margin.
