Implementation summary · Updated 21 September 2026

Calculators and worksheets

The site’s own code processes quote numbers and tour worksheet entries in your browser. It does not send these inputs to an AI provider or our server, and does not deliberately save them between visits. Printing or saving a PDF creates a copy under your control. Your browser may separately retain form information according to its settings.

Other data collection

There are no travel accounts, payment forms, newsletter forms or review submissions. The site’s own code contains no added advertising or analytics scripts. Fonts are loaded from your device.

Hosting and access

OpenAI Sites hosts this restricted-access edition and may require a platform sign-in. Hosting and security infrastructure may process connection or account information separately. We have not completed an assessment of its logs, cookies, retention or processing locations.

External websites

External links take you to services with their own privacy practices. Do not enter sensitive personal details into the comparison worksheet.

Before broader data collection

The responsible operator and contact channel must be published, data flows assessed and applicable privacy requirements addressed. This implementation summary is not a claim of completed legal compliance.
