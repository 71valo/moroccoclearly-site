Casablanca tram and busway fares

What a tram or busway ride costs, which ticket to buy for your number of journeys, and when to validate.

The facts on this page were reviewed against their sources between 24 and 28 September 2026. None has been checked in person yet. Each fact shows its own status and date.

Fares

TicketPrice
One journey8 MAD Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources
Two journeys14 MAD Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources
Rechargeable card15 MAD for the card, then 6 MAD per journey Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources

These are the tram and busway fares. Do not assume they cover the airport Aérobus or ONCF trains.

Which ticket is cheaper

This is arithmetic on the prices above, for one person:

  • Two journeys: the two-journey ticket costs 14 MAD. A new card plus two journeys costs 27 MAD.
  • 8 journeys or more: a new card (15 + 8 × 6 = 63 MAD) is cheaper than eight single tickets (64 MAD).
  • 16 journeys or more: the card is also cheaper than buying two-journey tickets (7 MAD a journey).

Validate every time

The operator requires you to validate when you enter, when you exit, and when you enter during a connection Source-checked 24 Sep 2026 Sources. Keep your ticket until you have left the station.

Before you ride

Our advice: plan from your real start and end points, not just the nearest stop. Include the walk at each end, and check the last departure from your stop before an evening out.
