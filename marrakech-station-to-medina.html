Marrakech station to the medina

Read the stop on your ticket. The train list names Marrakech in Guéliz and prints no street. A coach may stop at Bab Doukkala, very close to a main medina gate, or on another street. A city-bus trip is printed at 4 MAD, with no line from the station.

SHORT ANSWER

Read the stop on your ticket, name the gate your place gave you, and ask for the meter before any bag goes in the boot.

Neither language gives a street, a minute count, or a fare from that list to a gate.

Step by step

Our advice: read the stop on the ticket before you look for a car. Guéliz and Bab Doukkala are both real names on these pages. Treat them as two stops until the ticket shows they are the same one.

  1. If you came by train, the stop the list names is MARRAKECH Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources, and that record has no street Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. The council's Guéliz page puts the station in Guéliz Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. A second ONCF stop is the stadium, SIDI BRAHIM MARRAKECH STADIUM Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. If the ticket says the stadium, that is the other stop. These pages do not place it at a medina gate.
  2. If you came by coach, start with the name on the ticket. The transport pages, in English and in French, place the coach station at Bab Doukkala Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources and say tickets are sold inside the station Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. The gate page calls Bab Doukkala a main way into the medina Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources and the station very close Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. The same Guéliz list, in English, says Marrakech bus and train station Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources, and in French it says Gare routière et ferroviaire de Marrakech Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. Supratours lists Bab Doukkala Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources on Avenue Mly El Hassan Bel Mahdi Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources, Avenue Hassan II Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources by the train station, and number 2, Lot Zaitoun, Amerchiche Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. The events page puts that Hassan II counter Avenue Hassan II in Guéliz Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources.
  3. CTM's own list says Boulevard Aboubaker Seddik Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources at Zineb Nefzaouia Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. The events page says 12 boulevard Zerktouni Sources disagree Sources. Those streets do not match. Go to the street written on your ticket, and ask which door if the two still look different.
  4. For a city bus, the council prints 4 MAD Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources a trip and still calls the network ALSA Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. It names no line from the train station or the coach station to a medina gate Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. A press account, not opened again for this page, says Supratours was to start Marrakech urban and peri-urban buses on 14 December 2025, after ALSA Unconfirmed Sources, and it gives No fare in dirhams, and no airport line number, in those articles Unconfirmed Sources. Supratours' site does not show a Marrakech city-bus timetable Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. Do not pick a bus only because the council page says ALSA. If no line is posted at the door, use a taxi and ask for the meter. Do not treat the airport bus as this ride. That airport price, and the hours printed for it, stay on Marrakech airport to the medina.
  5. For a taxi, the council names stations near the Koutoubia, at Sidi Mimoun and at Bab Doukkala Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources and says a ride there can be negotiated Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. It does not name a rank at the train-station door. Ask for the meter before you sit down. Le compteur, s'il vous plaît, avant de partir. The meter, please, before we leave. The English tourist-office page says rates are regulated and taxis have meters Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. The French page opened from it does not say taxis have meters Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. Ask anyway. The city steps are on Petit taxis in Marrakech.
  6. Say which ride you want. Cars at the stations named above that leave once the car is full Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources are the shared ride toward other towns. If you are going to the medina, say so before you get in. Je vais à la médina, pas vers une autre ville. I am going to the medina, not to another town. إلى المدينة القديمة، من فضلك، وليس إلى مدينة أخرى. To the old medina, please, not to another town.
  7. Name the gate, not only the house. Whether a car can drive the last lanes is not confirmed. Agree the stop before the door shuts. An app, when a car is offered, is Uber and ride apps in Marrakech. Those pages do not say an app car can collect you at the train station or at Bab Doukkala.
  8. Carry dirham notes. What the notes look like, and where to get them at the airport, is Cash and ATMs at Marrakech airport. If the ticket lives on your phone, a line is SIM card at Marrakech airport. If the train started at Casa Voyageurs, that station is Catching your train at Casa Voyageurs, and the rides are Casablanca to Marrakech by train and Marrakech to Fes by train. The edge case

You arrive after dark. The gate page says the old doors used to close in the evening and stay open now Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. It does not print a last bus, and it does not print the hour a night taxi rate would start. The airport night price belongs to the airport ride, on Marrakech airport to the medina. Do not reuse it for this hop.

Our advice: after dark, ask for the meter or agree a price before you get in. Do not count on a city bus until you have seen that night's board.

The English useful-numbers page says Central Station Sources disagree Sources where the French page says Poste centrale Sources disagree Sources. The postal lines place the main post office at Place du 16 novembre Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources in Guéliz, and another at Jemaâ El Fna Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. Follow the train list, not that English label.

Ramadan is not mentioned on these station lines. A day when the trains or the coaches stop is not on these pages either. If the ticket says SIDI BRAHIM MARRAKECH STADIUM Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources, that is the other ONCF stop. These pages do not place it at a medina gate.

What to watch for

Our advice: the surprise is usually the wrong door, or a price borrowed from a different ride. Settle both before the car moves.

  • The airport prices. Day and night figures for the airport taxi, and the airport bus, stay on Marrakech airport to the medina. They are not a fare from Guéliz or from Bab Doukkala.
  • Two CTM streets. The operator list says Boulevard Aboubaker Seddik Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. The events page says 12 boulevard Zerktouni Sources disagree Sources. Your ticket is the one to follow.
  • A shared car to another town. At the named stations a car may leave once the car is full Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. That is the ride out of town. Say you are staying in the medina if that is the car waiting.
  • The English words Central Station. On the useful-numbers page they sit where French says Poste centrale Sources disagree Sources. The post office is not the train stop.
  • A named price with the meter dark. That price is the offer. Agree it, or wait, before any bag goes in the boot. A large note that cannot be broken is a payment problem, not a new fare.
  • Three extra names on the coach list. Gare de Marrakech Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources, Centre-ville de Marrakech Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources and the airport row are not the ONCF stop named MARRAKECH Source-checked 27 Oct 2026 Sources. What they are for is still open.
  • The walk at the end. Agree the gate before you set off. A fare written down at the kerb belongs in the fare log. There are no rows yet.
