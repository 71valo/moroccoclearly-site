How an entry is recorded

We write down the quote on the spot, with the date, city, route, the price in dirhams, how many people and bags, what the price covered, and whether it was agreed before the trip began. Where it helps, we add a photo of a rate sign or a drop-off point, never of a person.

What we never do

  • Average quotes, give ranges, or publish a price we did not hear ourselves.
  • Name, photograph or rate a driver or a business.
  • Call a higher price a scam. We write what was quoted, and when.

Reading one quote

Our advice: a quote shows what was asked on one day, for one trip. Use it to know what to ask and what to agree before you set off, not as a fixed fare.

Our guides show the fares published by operators, such as the airport train and Aérobus. This log is where prices we heard ourselves are kept.
