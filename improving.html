Trust takes work.
Here is ours.

Our goal is to be useful enough that you feel comfortable recommending us. More pages alone won’t achieve that. Better evidence, accountable people and listening to travelers will.

These are planned improvements, not completed research or promised launch dates.

What changed on 29 September 2026

Four Casablanca guides, where every price, time or rule shows a status badge, the date it was checked and its source. A fare log for prices quoted to us in person. A new design, with its own fonts and a dark mode. Corrections to pages that described our hosting and our editor out of date.

Changes on 21 September 2026

A clearer navigation, practical guide hub, separate standards and privacy pages, and a guided tour comparison worksheet.

What we are exploring

Our focus is dated, sourced guides, starting with Casablanca. We are also exploring tools that help travellers check a trip plan. Any such tool would be tested with travellers first and would show its sources. No AI service is connected yet.

01
PLANNED

Put real accountability behind the name.

The editor, Omar Oujoud, is now named on every page. Still to do: publish a monitored contact for corrections. Explain the editor’s experience and where contributors have actually visited.

What completion should look like

You can identify who is responsible, submit an error and see when a material correction was made. Contact details and response arrangements must be tested before we advertise them.

02
IN PROGRESS

Document one arrival experience properly.

We started with Casablanca airport. The guides now show operator fares and times with their dates and sources. Still to do in person: the first train of the day, the walk from arrivals to the platform, the Aérobus stops and a step-free route through Casa Voyageurs, with original photographs.

What completion should look like

Every route includes the date checked, who checked it, the source of each practical detail and any access limitations. Missing or unconfirmed details remain labeled.

03
STARTED

Publish prices with their context.

The fare log is live, with no quotes recorded yet. Each entry will be one price quoted to us in person, with its date, route, group size, bags, what was included and whether it was agreed before leaving. Operator fares stay in the guides, each with its source.

What completion should look like

Quotes are shown one by one, never averaged or turned into a range, and never described as a fair or unfair price. Every quote keeps its date, so you can judge how current it is.

04
PLANNED

Learn from real travelers.

Ask first-time visitors to use the arrival guide and show where they still get stuck. Include different budgets, arrival times and mobility needs.

What completion should look like

Publish what was confusing and what changed. Count successful tasks and useful corrections before celebrating page views. Any future feedback collection needs a clear purpose and privacy information.

05
BEFORE A WIDER COMMERCIAL LAUNCH

Make the business worthy of the trust.

Confirm the business identity, review hosting and personal-data handling, and complete applicable legal steps. Have the exact service model assessed before arranging bookings or taking commissions.

What completion should look like

Accurate privacy information and service terms, a working complaints route, and visible commercial disclosures if partnerships are introduced. Payment must never buy an editorial verdict.

HOW WE WANT TO WORK

Goodwill comes from
being useful.

Keep practical help accessible. Explain uncertainty. Treat travelers and local businesses fairly. Correct mistakes openly. Let a recommendation be earned.
