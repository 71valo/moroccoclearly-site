Our goal is to be useful enough that you feel comfortable recommending us. More pages alone won’t achieve that. Better evidence, accountable people and listening to travelers will.

These are planned improvements, not completed research or promised launch dates.

01
PLANNED

Put real accountability behind the name.

Publish the operator’s identity, an accountable editor and a monitored contact for corrections. Explain the editor’s experience and where contributors have actually visited.

What completion should look like

You can identify who is responsible, submit an error and see when a material correction was made. Contact details and response arrangements must be tested before we advertise them.

02
RESEARCH NEEDED

Document one arrival experience properly.

Start with Marrakech airport to accommodation. Gather original photographs, check pickup instructions, document final walking access and compare available transport options.

What completion should look like

Every route includes the date checked, who checked it, the source of each practical detail and any access limitations. Missing or unconfirmed details remain labeled.

03
RESEARCH NEEDED

Publish prices with their context.

Collect multiple comparable observations. Separate official tariffs, written provider quotes and traveler reports. Include route, date, group size, currency, inclusions and extra charges.

What completion should look like

A range has a visible sample size and collection period. It is described as observed pricing, not a guaranteed fair price. Old observations are rechecked or marked stale.

04
PLANNED

Learn from real travelers.

Ask first-time visitors to use the arrival guide and show where they still get stuck. Include different budgets, arrival times and mobility needs.

What completion should look like

Publish what was confusing and what changed. Count successful tasks and useful corrections before celebrating page views. Any future feedback collection needs a clear purpose and privacy information.

05
BEFORE A WIDER COMMERCIAL LAUNCH

Make the business worthy of the trust.

Confirm the business identity, review hosting and personal-data handling, and complete applicable legal steps. Have the exact service model assessed before arranging bookings or taking commissions.

What completion should look like

Accurate privacy information and service terms, a working complaints route, and visible commercial disclosures if partnerships are introduced. Payment must never buy an editorial verdict.

HOW WE WANT TO WORK

Goodwill comes from
being useful.

Keep practical help accessible. Explain uncertainty. Treat travelers and local businesses fairly. Correct mistakes openly. Let a recommendation be earned.
