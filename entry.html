Entry checks: start with your documents, not someone else's experience

Find the official entry process for your own documents and circumstances.

Updated 21 September 2026 · Original preparation advice and limited official-source summaries. No local field verification.

Short answer: Use the official eligibility service for your circumstances. Another traveler's experience does not establish what applies to you.

Prepare the details the official form asks for

The Accès Maroc eligibility form includes nationality, country of residence, passport type, travel purpose and whether you hold another valid visa. These details help explain why a general yes-or-no answer can be misleading.

A practical sequence

  1. Gather the relevant details about your own documents and trip.
  2. Open the official Accès Maroc service and follow its eligibility process.
  3. Use Moroccan consular services if you need clarification about your circumstances.
  4. Separately check any requirements for countries you transit through.

Do not send passport scans or identity numbers to Morocco, Clearly to use this guide. Enter personal information only where necessary in the official process.

Next step: Open Accès Maroc.
