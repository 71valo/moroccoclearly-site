If Jardin Majorelle is sold out

What to check first, and three places nearby with their hours and entry fares.

SHORT ANSWER

Look at another day on the garden’s own shop before you give up. The garden is open daily 08:00–18:30, last entry 18:00 Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources.

If that day is full, the YSL Museum lists 140 MAD Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources and Dar El Bacha lists 60 MAD for foreign visitors Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources.

Buy each ticket on that place’s own site. Wednesday is a poor swap for the museum: it is closed.

Step by step

Our advice: look at other days and times on the garden’s own shop, reached from the garden’s homepage. Buy only there, so the ticket comes from the garden itself. The shop address to expect is tickets.jardinmajorelle.com.

The garden is open daily 08:00–18:30, last entry 18:00 Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources.

Our advice: check each museum’s own website for how to buy entry on your date. museeyslmarrakech.com for the YSL Museum. fnm.ma for Dar El Bacha.

The edge case

The YSL Museum is closed on Wednesday. Dar El Bacha is closed Monday Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources. If the garden hour you wanted was a Wednesday, the museum is not the replacement. If it was a Monday, Dar El Bacha is not either.

Our advice: once you know a date that works, book it on the garden’s own shop, and keep the ticket on your phone.

What to watch for

Our advice: a sold-out message on a site that is not the garden’s shop may be that site’s own stock. Check the garden’s shop before you change the day.

  • A similar web address. The shop linked from the homepage is the one that sells the garden’s ticket.
  • Assuming the Pierre Bergé Museum is included. Its entry price is not confirmed here, and neither is whether you need a garden ticket to go in.
  • A Wednesday museum plan. The YSL Museum is closed that day.
