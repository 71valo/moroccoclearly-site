If Jardin Majorelle is sold out

What to check first, and three places nearby with their hours and entry fares.

Written by Omar Oujoud in Casablanca. Research drafting is AI-assisted; observations are recorded in person. How we check

The facts on this page were reviewed against their sources on 28 September 2026. None has been checked in person yet. Each fact shows its own status and date.

First, check again

Our advice: look at other days and times on the garden’s own shop, tickets.jardinmajorelle.com, reached from the garden’s homepage. Buy only there, so your ticket comes from the garden itself.

The garden is open daily 08:00–18:30, last entry 18:00 Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources.

Our advice: once you know a date that works, book it straight away on the garden’s own shop, and keep the ticket on your phone.
