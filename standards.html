Where our latest guides come from

The train guide uses limited factual information from ONCF ticket conditions. The arrival guide points to ONDA airport assistance information. The entry guide explains the fields in the official Accès Maroc eligibility form. Each guide links to its source and describes what was checked.

Some ONCF and ONDA information was accessible through indexed primary-source extracts rather than a direct live page. We disclose that limitation and do not present prices, schedules, pickup points or eligibility as verified. Check the authority’s own service before acting.

Our checklists and suggested questions are original editorial guidance. These guides do not reproduce third-party photographs, complete articles or policy documents, and do not imply endorsement by the linked organizations.

Different kinds of information

Official information: follow the linked authority for current requirements. Provider statements: written terms are evidence of what was promised, not independent verification. Editorial guidance: preparation questions and suggestions reflect judgment.

Dates have a meaning

A page update records an editorial change. A source-review date records an online check. Neither means someone inspected a location. We do not claim local field verification in this edition.

What our tools do now

The quote calculator performs arithmetic. The tour worksheet organizes the terms you enter and identifies fields you left blank. Neither reads an offer automatically, verifies a business or recommends a provider using AI.

How we intend to use AI

A future assistant should explain tradeoffs using your priorities and visible evidence. It should distinguish quoted terms from interpretation, ask when information is missing and allow “not enough information.” It must not invent prices, availability or safety assurances. Accountability is still a gap

A public operator identity, named editor and monitored corrections contact still need to be established. See our contact status and improvement plan. We do not present this early edition as a fully verified travel authority.
