Jardin Majorelle: tickets, hours and nearby museums

How to buy a ticket from the garden’s own shop, when it’s open, and the museums to plan around it.

The facts on this page were reviewed against their sources on 28 September 2026. None has been checked in person yet. Each fact shows its own status and date.

Buying a ticket

Tickets are sold online, through the ticket shop linked from the garden’s own homepage Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources. That shop is at tickets.jardinmajorelle.com.

Our advice: start from the garden’s own homepage, jardinmajorelle.com, and follow its ticket link. Before you pay, check that the address bar reads tickets.jardinmajorelle.com. Other websites with similar names are not the garden’s own shop.

Our advice: if your dates are fixed, book as soon as you know them, and keep the ticket on your phone where it opens without mobile data.

Opening hours

The garden is open daily 08:00–18:30, last entry 18:00 Source-checked 28 Sep 2026 Sources.

Our advice: hours can change during Ramadan and on holidays. Check the garden’s own homepage for your date.

Access

The garden’s website makes a general statement about access for visitors with reduced mobility Unconfirmed Sources. It does not describe step-free routes in detail.

Our advice: if you need step-free access, ask the garden directly before booking a time.
