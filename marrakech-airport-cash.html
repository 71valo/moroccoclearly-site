Cash and ATMs at Marrakech airport

Where Menara lists an exchange counter, what a night landing does to passport control, and what is still missing for ATMs, counter hours and the rate.

SHORT ANSWER

Menara's aeronautical sheet lists an exchange office at the airport Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. That row gives no opening hours Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources and no cash machine Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources.

The airport operator's exchange pages put counters in the public hall Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. Some of those counters are also listed in the customs area Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. Neither page gives opening hours Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources.

Passport control is listed as H24 Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources, so a night landing still reaches the hall.

Change a small amount there if a counter is open, and keep the slip. The dirham fares for the taxi and for bus 19 are printed on Marrakech airport to the medina. Read them before you decide how much to change.

Dirham notes you carry across the border are described as no more than 2,000 MAD Unconfirmed Sources. That page did not open on this check. The same line is on Entry checks.

A phone line, if you still need one, is in SIM card at Marrakech airport.

How we checked

The exchange line was read in the Morocco AIP PDF for Menara, sheet AD 2.5, opened in a browser. The header on that sheet is 26 December 2023 Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. The French column names an exchange counter at the airport, then a post office. The English column names an exchange office at the airport, then a post office. Same row, no hours, no rate, no cash machine. The two languages do not disagree.

The hours sheet is AD 2.3. Its header is 26 December 2024 Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. Customs and immigration are H24 Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources in both language columns. A different row on that sheet is the aerodrome administration. This page does not print that row's clock. A screen reading was not steady on the first digit of the weekday start, so the clock stays off this page. It is not the counter's hours in any case.

The operator's French and English exchange pages did not open. A browser request returned a block page. The earlier record still stands: counters in the public hall, some of them also in the customs area, and no hours. Names on that page are not repeated.

The central bank's foreign-banknote page was opened in English and in French. The two languages showed the same figures, and the date box on the page was the same. That host is not linked from here, so the figures are not printed. The page does not say a counter at Menara has to use them.

Office des Changes did not open, in a browser or by a direct request. The dirham-note line and the declaration line stay the earlier records.

The taxi and bus figures in dirhams stay on the ride guide. They are not copied onto this page. Nothing here was seen at the airport.

Step by step

Our advice: do the money before you leave the hall. A counter you have walked past is harder to find again with a suitcase.

  1. You clear the border first. Customs and immigration are listed as H24 Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. A late flight does not, on this sheet, find that row closed.
  2. The passenger sheet lists an exchange office at the airport Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources and a post office at the airport Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. A post office is not the counter that buys your notes. The operator's pages put counters in the public hall Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources and say some are also listed in the customs area Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. If you are already in the public hall, ask there.
  3. Before you hand the notes over, ask to see the rate and the dirham total. If they write a slip, keep it. What that slip does when you leave Morocco is not on these pages.
  4. Open Marrakech airport to the medina and read the taxi figure and the bus figure. Change enough for the ride you will actually take, plus a little for the first hour. This page does not repeat those figures.
  5. A 2023 Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources announcement describes a taxi desk at the arrivals exit where you pay by card or cash before the ride Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. It says the computer system runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. It gives no fare, and it does not say a foreign card will work.
  6. The rank itself is described as a taxi station on the esplanade Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. If you want a phone line before that walk, use SIM card at Marrakech airport. Landing at the other city is Casablanca airport. A cash guide for that terminal is not written yet.

Phrases, if you need them:

  • Le cours, et le montant en dirhams, avant de changer, s'il vous plaît. The rate, and the amount in dirhams, before I change money, please.
  • من فضلك، أريد أن أرى سعر الصرف والمبلغ بالدرهم قبل أن أبدل. I want to see the rate and the dirham amount before I change money.

The edge case

You land late, the hall is quiet, and no counter is staffed. The passenger sheet still lists the exchange office, and it still gives no opening hours Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. It still names no cash machine Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. Passport control is still listed as H24 Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources. That gets you into the hall. It does not open a counter.

The taxi desk in the 2023 Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources announcement is described as taking card or cash Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources on a system that runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. Whether that desk is still there, and whether your card will be accepted, is not in the announcement. The ride guide is where the printed taxi and bus figures live, including the night taxi figure. Read it before you leave the hall. If the counter was shut, you are relying on notes you already hold, or on a card at that desk.

Ramadan is not mentioned on the exchange row. The administration row on the hours sheet has a Ramadan line. This page does not print it, and it does not belong to the counter.

Our advice: if the counter is dark, do not hunt the building for a machine these pages never name. Use the notes you have, or the card, for the ride, and change money once you are in town.

What to watch for

Our advice: the decision is the dirham total you can read before your notes leave your hand.

  • Two zones, one list. Some counters are listed in the customs area and in the public hall. The pages do not say which door you use on the way in, or on the way out. Once you are in the public hall, start there.
  • A post office on the same row. The passenger sheet lists a post office at the airport Source-checked 26 Oct 2026 Sources beside the exchange office. A post office does not buy your notes.
  • A rate you only see afterwards. Ask for the dirham total first. A higher total than the one you hoped for is a price you were offered. It is not a reason to describe the person.
  • The administration clock. The hours sheet has a row for the aerodrome administration. It is not the exchange office. This page leaves that clock unstated on purpose.
  • Home currency at the taxi desk. The announcement says card or cash. It does not say the desk will charge you in the currency of your card. Read the dirham figure on the ride guide, and the figure on any screen the desk shows you.
  • Notes you brought from home. The border record says no more than 2,000 MAD Unconfirmed Sources. Foreign cash of 100,000 MAD or more Unconfirmed Sources is the declaration line in the other record. Neither page opened on this check.
  • The other airport. Menara is RAK. Mohammed V, at Casablanca, is a different terminal. Do not use this page for that arrival.

  • The weekday clock and the Ramadan line on the aerodrome administration row. A screen reading was not steady on the first digit of the weekday start, so they are not printed here. They are not the counter's hours.

  • The dirham amount a counter pays for one euro, one US dollar or one pound, and whether the counter uses the central bank's banknote rate. That rate was read in English and in French and is not printed here.
  • Whether a counter listed in the customs area is reachable on arrival, on departure, or both.
  • What a counter asks you to show, and whether the slip lets you change dirhams back at the airport when you fly home.
