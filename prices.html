THE QUOTE COMPARISON TOOL

What will your
group actually pay?

Enter two quotes in the same currency. Add extras as a total for the whole group—even when the main price is per person.

This calculates costs from your numbers. It does not check market rates, provider quality or whether a price is fair.

A quick example

Four people × 150 MAD = 600 MAD. A 500 MAD group quote with 50 MAD of extras totals 550 MAD.

Both quotes must use this currency. No exchange-rate conversion is performed.

MAKE THE COMPARISON MEANINGFUL

Same experience. Same details.

Transfers

Confirm the exact pickup and destination, vehicle capacity, luggage, waiting allowance and what happens if your flight is late.

Ask whether the final drop-off is at your door or a nearby accessible road.

Day trips

Check departure and return times, private versus shared transport, stops, entry fees and meals.

A longer list of destinations may mean less time in the place you most want to visit.

Guides

Agree on the duration, language, meeting point, group size and route. Ask whether shopping stops are optional.

Check credentials and cancellation terms directly before you commit.

A MESSAGE YOU CAN REUSE

Ask clearly.
Compare confidently.

Replace the brackets with your trip details. Send the same request to each provider.

Hello! Could you quote for [service and route] on [date], for [number of travelers] with [luggage or specific needs]? Please confirm the full total and currency, whether it is per person or for the group, what is included, any additional charges, the pickup and drop-off arrangements, and your payment and cancellation terms. Thank you!

A useful number still needs context.

The lowest total is not automatically the best choice. Check recent independent reviews, credentials where relevant and written terms. Keep a record of the agreement. Never send payment just because a quote appears in a comparison.

Morocco’s currency is the dirham (MAD). For official practical information, see Visit Morocco.
