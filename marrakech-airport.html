Marrakech airport to the medina, day and night

Bus, taxi or ride app from Menara airport (RAK) to the medina: what is posted, what a late landing changes, and the walk from the gate.

Written by Omar Oujoud in Casablanca. Research drafting is AI-assisted; observations are recorded in person. How we check

The facts on this page were reviewed against their sources between 30 September and 20 October 2026. None has been checked in person yet. Each fact shows its own status and date.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Our advice: with bags, use the taxi rank on the esplanade and the destination fare posted there, and settle that fare before the bags go in. The airport’s own page also describes a city bus every 20 minutes Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources, and it does not give a fare or a last departure. Ask your riad which gate a car can reach: the ride ends where a car can stop, and the rest is a walk.

Step by step

Which airport, and how far

Both presentation pages give the code RAK Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. The French page places the airport 3 km from the city centre Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. The English page does not give a distance. Neither page says that the city centre is Jemaa el-Fna.

The airport bus

Both language versions of the taxi and bus page describe one bus between the city and the airport, every 20 minutes Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. They do not name the stop, the price of a ticket, who sells it, or a first or last departure. Those pages are undated apart from a copyright line.

Who runs the buses now is reported in the press, not on a timetable those pages link. Supratours, an ONCF subsidiary, reported as starting city buses on 14 December 2025, after ALSA Unconfirmed Sources. A later article said Marrakech Mobility had told the paper the takeover had happened toward the end of that December, and that a gazette text of 15 December 2025 Unconfirmed Sources authorised SGTUPM, a company held by ONCF and Supratours Unconfirmed Sources, to manage the network. No fare in dirhams, and no airport line number, in those articles Unconfirmed Sources. One of them said fares then in force kept until the end of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations Unconfirmed Sources, with changes already decided to follow. It did not state either price.

Supratours’ own site is an intercity coach planner, not a city or airport bus timetable Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources.

Our advice: if you take the bus, pay the way the stop tells you to. Do not count on a departure after dark until you have seen that day’s board. Bus prices that appear only on other websites are not repeated here.

A taxi from the rank

The airport’s page puts a taxi station on the esplanade and says destination fares are displayed at the taxi parking. It does not print the amounts, the hours of any night fare, or whether the price is for the whole car. The board has not been read for this guide, so prices from other websites are not repeated. A taxi fare is added to the fare log only once it has been recorded at the rank.

Separately, ONDA published a news item on 12 May 2023 Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. A desk at the arrivals exit: pay by card or cash before the ride, then scan a receipt for a ticket with your taxi number Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. The announcement says those fares are a flat amount for the destination, set by the competent authorities, and that the computer system runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. It gives no amount. It says signs after baggage claim lead to the desk. The taxi and bus page does not mention this desk, and an English version of the news item was not found. That the desk is still there has not been confirmed.

Our advice: do it in this order:

  1. Walk past offers of a ride inside the terminal.
  2. Follow the signs to the desk or the rank, and read the posted fare for your destination before you agree.
  3. If there is a desk, pay there and keep the receipt.
  4. Name the gate your riad gave you, not only the name of the house.
  5. Carry dirhams as well as a card. Whether a foreign card works at the desk at night is not yet confirmed.

Phrases, if you need them:

  • « Le tarif affiché pour cette destination, s’il vous plaît. » The posted fare for this destination, please.
  • من فضلك، ما الثمن المكتوب لهذه الوجهة؟ The same question in Arabic.

A ride app

Uber’s newsroom, in English and in French, says the app is offered in Marrakech and Casablanca, only with licensed transport vehicles, as UberX and UberXL Unconfirmed Sources. Neither page says whether a car may collect a rider at Menara Unconfirmed Sources. Whether an app can pick up at the terminal is not yet confirmed.

Morocco’s Ministry of Transport has been reported as saying that carrying paying passengers without a transport licence, or in a private car, is an offence Unconfirmed Sources.

Our advice: for a first arrival, the rank is the simpler choice until pickup at the terminal is confirmed. If you do request a car, use the pickup point the app shows, and check that the vehicle is a licensed taxi or transport vehicle before you get in.

From the gate to the door

There is no sourced list here of medina gates a car can use, or a walking time from any gate to a door.

Our advice: before you fly, ask your accommodation which gate a car should use, how long the walk is with your bags, and whether someone can meet you there if you land late. The same questions are on the Marrakech guide.

The edge case

Landing late

The airport page gives no first or last bus. The desk announcement describes a system running 24 hours a day, 7 days a week Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. The taxi and bus page does not say the rank itself runs all night.

Our advice: if you might land after dark, plan as if the bus has finished. Passport control and baggage can take a while. Use the rank, or a pickup your accommodation has put in writing, including what happens if the flight is late. The airport’s exchange page lists counters in the public hall and gives no opening hours Source-checked 20 Oct 2026 Sources. Whether those counters, or the cash machines, are open at night is not yet confirmed. Get dirhams before you leave the terminal if you can.

A full car, or a door the car cannot reach

Our advice: before any bag goes in, ask whether your group and your luggage fit in one car, and what the posted fare includes. If the riad is inside the medina, the fare on the board may end at a gate. The walk from that gate is part of the journey.

What to watch for

Our advice: these habits avoid most surprises:

  • Offers inside the terminal. The fare to compare is the one posted at the rank or charged at the desk.
  • A price that is not on the list. Ask to see it on the list before you agree.
  • Settling the price once you are moving. Agree it before the car leaves.
  • A stop that is not the gate you named. Show the gate name you were given. A car that stops short of that gate is a different journey.
  • Large notes. Carry smaller notes. Change can be hard to find.
  • A site that sells the ride. Menara’s transport pages are on onda.ma. A page that sells a transfer is a seller.
