Who publishes this site

Morocco, Clearly, at moroccoclearly.com, is written and edited by Omar Oujoud in Casablanca. Research drafting is AI-assisted. Any observation published in the fare log is recorded in person and dated.

The site is independent: not affiliated with the Moroccan government or the Moroccan National Tourist Office, and not affiliated with a transport operator, museum or other business named in the guides.

What kind of site this is

This is a free, non-commercial information site: practical guides and tools for visitors. There are no bookings, payments, advertisements, paid placements, affiliate links or commissions. We do not take reservations or arrange travel for a fee.

Editorial responsibility

Omar Oujoud is responsible for what is published. In the guides, a price, time or rule carries a status badge and a link to its source. A paragraph of advice is marked as our judgement. A source-review date means the page was read online on that day. It does not mean someone visited the place.
