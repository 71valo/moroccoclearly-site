Booking a train? Check flexibility as well as price

Check the journey and ticket conditions before choosing a train fare.

Updated 21 September 2026 · Original preparation advice and limited official-source summaries. No local field verification.

Short answer: Choose a ticket around how certain your plans are. Before paying, check the exact journey and the conditions attached to your selected fare.

What the official information tells us

ONCF publishes different exchange conditions for different fare types. Its conditions also make exchanges subject to availability. A cheaper ticket and a more flexible ticket may therefore solve different needs.

Your booking checklist

  • Confirm the departure and arrival stations, date and time.
  • Read the exchange and cancellation terms for the actual fare selected.
  • Check the passenger details before payment.
  • Keep the booking confirmation and instructions for presenting the ticket.

Make the journey fit your trip

Our planning suggestion: allow time to reach the station and get from the arrival station to your accommodation. If connecting from a flight, consider the consequences of a delay before choosing a restrictive train fare. We do not suggest a universal connection buffer: it depends on the journey and your tolerance for disruption.

Next step: Check journeys with ONCF and read the conditions displayed before purchase.

Source: ONCF ticket conditions. Specific exchange details were available through indexed official-source text on 21 September 2026. Direct homepage access failed during research. No live departure, fare or availability was verified; this guide deliberately avoids fixed prices and schedules.
