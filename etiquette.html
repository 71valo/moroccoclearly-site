Small questions. Better interactions.

Thoughtful preparation for everyday moments, without assuming everyone has the same preferences.

Editorial preparation guide · Updated 21 September 2026 · No local field checks claimed.

Ask before photographing people

Seek permission and accept a refusal. If a photograph or experience involves payment, agree the terms beforehand.

Make help and prices clear

If someone offers a service, clarify whether it is paid and agree the price before accepting. A simple “No, thank you” is enough when you do not want it.

Follow guidance at individual places

Check access rules, photography restrictions and dress expectations at the venue you plan to visit. Ask the host or staff if you are unsure.

Leave room for individual differences

Listen, ask politely and avoid treating a single encounter as representative of a whole country. Confirm opening arrangements for your specific dates rather than assuming a normal schedule.

Something looks wrong?

See our contact and corrections status. Confirm changing details with the source or provider before relying on them.
